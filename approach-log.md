**Follow-up from H3 and H4: why did the four growing cities still miss plan?** The plan is a flat uplift on each city's 2025 volume (plan ÷ 2025 tests is constant across months). Given the new patients they actually won, returning-patient tests needed +15.0% to +20.3% growth to hit plan; they grew +0.1% to +3.0%. About 90% of H1 2026 returning patients were active in 2025, and that base was flat (slightly smaller in H2 2025 than in H1 2025 in all four cities), with a steady 46–48% return rate from one half to the next. The gap was built into the plan's assumption, not caused by an operating failure. Limit: no bookings before 2025.

**Second follow-up: the January 2026 step change.** New patients rose about 17% a month in the four cities and fell about 26% (Chennai) and 34% (Jaipur) from January 2026, holding for all six months. Campaign, channel, source, duplicate registrations, wrong-city records and signup-date recording were all ruled out. The cause is unresolved.
